3. THE DEEP PHYSICS OF TOKENIZATION, CHUNKING, AND VECTOR POOLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To outperform these six papers deterministically, D-COMP-RAG enforces three physical laws:

(A) Subword Tokenization Non-Linearity (Byte-Pair Encoding Physics):
    - The Prefix-Space Boundary Law: In Rust tokenizers like Qwen2TokenizerFast (151,643 vocab),
      BPE merges include leading whitespace (Ġ or ▁). Slicing at an arbitrary character index 
      without aligning to token boundaries splits " It" into different token IDs than "It", 
      shifting the initial embedding lookup \vec{e}_i and breaking embedding invariance.
    - Numeric & Table Shattering: Prose averages ~4 characters per token, but financial tables 
      with symbols ($, |, %, ., -) fragment into 1–2 characters per token. A 600-character 
      table row consumes 3x more tokens than a 600-character paragraph. Naive character chunkers 
      silently overrun the 512-token transformer ceiling and drop columns.
    - Bijective Byte-Offset Ledger: The chunker must maintain a strict surjective mapping from 
      UTF-8 byte spans [b_start, b_end) to token indices [t_start, t_end) via return_offsets_mapping=True:
          \bigcup_{k} [b_start^{(k)}, b_end^{(k)}) = [0, len(D))
      guaranteeing 100.00% non-whitespace byte preservation.

(B) Mean-Pooling Centroid Drift & Signal Dilution:
    Let a chunk have N active tokens with final-layer hidden states \vec{h}_1, ..., \vec{h}_N \in \mathbb{R}^d.
    Mean pooling computes the L2-normalized centroid:
        \vec{v}_chunk = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^N \vec{h}_i}{\|\sum_{i=1}^N \vec{h}_i\|_2}

    - The Pronoun Vacuum Effect (Jang et al., ACL 2025): If a chunk contains K coreferential 
      pronouns ("it", "they", "its"), their hidden vectors \vec{h}_pronoun lie near the generic 
      syntactic origin rather than the domain entity subspace \vec{u}_entity. The cosine similarity 
      with a query \vec{q} asking about the entity drops proportionally to K / N.
    - The Concatenation Dilution Law (Why SAKI-RAG Failed on Single-Entity Datasets):
      In SAKI-RAG, concatenating target sentence s_i with attention-linked sentences R_i produces:
          \vec{v}_{s_i \oplus R_i} \propto \sum_{t \in s_i} \vec{h}_t + \sum_{t \in R_i} \vec{h}_t
      When |R_i| >> |s_i|, the vectors from R_i dominate the numerator and pull the centroid 
      away from s_i, causing the true answer chunk's reranking score to collapse.
    - The Mathematical Fix: NEVER blindly concatenate whole neighboring chunks into the embedding text.
      Instead, inject only compact, high-density canonical headers (Breadcrumb + Unit Schema + 
      Resolved Entity Handle <= 45 tokens) so \vec{u}_entity reinforces the centroid without 
      diluting the local predicate.

(C) 2D Coordinate & Tree Topology Collapse:
    A document is a 2D Coordinate Grid (tables where cell c_{r,j} = f(R_r, C_j, U_unit)) 
    nested inside a Directed Tree (Doc -> Chapter -> Section -> Clause -> List Item).
    Indexing financial tables at the contiguous row-level with preserved headers/units achieves 
    100.00% accuracy (EnSI-RAG Table 4) vs. 91.50% when dumping the entire table as one chunk.
    Packing contiguous row blocks [r : r+k] preserves accounting identities 
    (Gross Profit - OpEx = Operating Income) while staying strictly under the 512-token ceiling.

6. THE CONJUNCTIVE EVIDENCE DISCONNECTION PROBLEM & PROOF-CARRYING CHUNKING (PCC)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(A) The Survival vs. Semantic Entanglement Paradox:
    "Information survives, but its meaning gets disconnected."
    Standard chunking benchmarks commit a fatal category error: they evaluate 
    syntactic preservation (character/token recall) and single-hop semantic similarity.
    However, 100% of words can survive while 100% of the conjunctive truth conditions 
    are destroyed!

    Consider a 100-page enterprise financial filing:
      - PAGE 1  (Fact A)     : "The company's operating income was 12.4."
      - PAGE 2  (Banner B)   : "The figures in this table are expressed in millions of USD."
      - PAGE 7  (Clause C)   : "The figures exclude discontinued operations."
      - PAGE 12 (Footnote D) : "The reported figures were later restated under a revised accounting policy."

    When split across separate chunks:
      - Zero words have been deleted.
      - Yet Chunk 1 alone is a semantic trap:
        * Is it $12.40 or $12,400,000? (Unit disconnect)
        * Does it include discontinued operations? (Scope/exclusion disconnect)
        * Is it valid or superseded? (Temporal restatement disconnect)
    An LLM retrieving Chunk 1 will generate answers with high confidence that are 
    grossly incorrect, legally hazardous, or economically catastrophic.

(B) The 5 Archetypes of Conjunctive Relationships Lost Across Pages:
    1. Scale & Dimensionality Grounding:
       Value v on Page p_1 <---> Unit multiplier / currency banner on Page p_2.
       (e.g., "12.4" vs. "$ USD Millions").
    2. Scope Conditions & Exclusion Grounding:
       Assertion on Page p_1 <---> Negative qualifier / exception clause on Page p_7.
       (e.g., "All operations" vs. "Excluding discontinued fabrication units").
    3. Temporal Supersession & Lifecycle Grounding:
       Historical figure on Page p_1 <---> Restatement / amendment on Page p_12.
       (e.g., Reported vs. Restated under ASC 606).
    4. Statutory & Structural Citation Grounding:
       Enforcement clause on Page p_15 <---> Definitional threshold on Page p_4.
       (e.g., "Threshold defined in Section 4.1 was breached").
    5. Nomenclature & Global Definitional Grounding:
       Entity handle on Page p_28 <---> Preamble party definition on Page p_1.
       (e.g., "The Company" vs. "Meridian Vault IP Holdings LLC").

(C) The Mathematical Formulation of Conjunctive Grounding:
    In formal semantics, an atomic factual claim \phi is not unconditionally true in isolation.
    Its truth value is a conjunction over its governing evidence conjuncts:
        \Phi = \phi_{fact} \land Q_{unit} \land Q_{scope} \land Q_{temporal} \land Q_{def}
    When document D is partitioned into chunks C = {c_1, ..., c_M}:
    Standard RAG evaluates:
        \mathbb{E}[Answer \mid q, c_1]
    whereas ground truth requires:
        \mathbb{E}[Answer \mid q, \bigwedge_{j} Q_j]
    When \exists j such that Q_j \notin c_1, the retrieved chunk induces a biased prior:
        \mathbb{E}[Answer \mid q, c_1] \neq \mathbb{E}[Answer \mid q, \Phi]

(D) The Solution: Proof-Carrying Chunking (PCC-RAG):
    Drawing on Proof-Carrying Code (Necula & Lee, 1996), a chunk is NOT merely a bag of text.
    Every chunk is compiled as a certified tuple:
        c_i = < T_i, \Pi_i >
    where T_i is the bit-for-bit unmutated source text, and \Pi_i is a Machine-Verifiable 
    Proof Certificate of Conjunctive Grounding:
        \Pi_i = < AST_Path, \Pi_{unit}, \Pi_{scope}, \Pi_{temporal}, \Pi_{entities} >

    - \Pi_{unit}: Deterministic byte-span pointer to governing unit schema (Page 2).
    - \Pi_{scope}: Deterministic pointers to active exclusion clauses (Page 7).
    - \Pi_{temporal}: Deterministic flags indicating restatement/supersession (Page 12).
    - \Pi_{entities}: Resolved entity bindings to the Global Document Registry (Page 1).

(E) Machine-Verifiable Conjunctive Auditing:
    Instead of brittle substring checks, the PCC Verifier executes an algorithmic audit:
        IsEveryFactGrounded(c_i) == True
    If an atomic numerical or predicate fact in c_i has ungrounded units or dangling 
    cross-page conditions, the compiler binds the proof certificate pointers into 
    the chunk's canonical header:
        [PROOF-CERTIFICATE: Unit=$ USD Millions (p.2) | Scope=Excl Discontinued (p.7) | Status=Restated (p.12)]
    This guarantees that whenever c_i is retrieved, the LLM receives the full conjunctive 
    evidence required for truthful interpretation, without needing to merge 100 pages of context!
